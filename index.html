Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm Daisymond Chiboora — a full-stack engineer and CTO who has spent the last eight years building the software infrastructure behind African fintech, trade and compliance systems. This letter isn't written for one posting; it's an introduction to the kind of problems I like solving and the track record I bring to them.

As Chief Technology Officer of Maveriq Technologies, I own architecture, security and delivery across a six-product SaaS suite spanning accounting, strategy execution, personal finance and risk. The flagship engagement — MaveriQ Accounting — grew from a single freelance build into an integration with the Zambia Revenue Authority's Smart Invoice (VSDC) e-invoicing standard, verified end-to-end in the tax authority's own sandbox. In parallel, I lead Stratify's SOC 2 Type I readiness programme: defining trust-service criteria, building the control matrix and risk register, and running the gap analysis that will carry it to certification.

Before that, I spent five years on cross-border infrastructure at the COMESA Secretariat, helping build the Virtual Trade Facilitation System — an electronic-seal network tracking fuel tankers and containerised cargo across 21 member states in real time. Earlier still, I wrote the Solidity contracts behind an African blockchain remittance network, built AI-driven recommendation engines, and shipped fintech products ranging from instant loans to telemedicine marketplaces.

What ties this together is a habit of owning a stack end-to-end and treating security as first-class engineering rather than an afterthought — TLS and certificate management for government integrations, OAuth2/JWT and Sanctum authentication, MFA and RBAC design, and routine dependency auditing on every production service I run. I'd welcome the chance to discuss how that experience could serve your team; my CV is one click away, with the full detail behind each of these projects.
